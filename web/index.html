Templates

Seven looks, chosen by the story.

The AI picks a landscape look from the news and your brief, and you can switch it before the voice is recorded. Shorts & Reels is the vertical version for phones.

Lead Desk

The presenter beside the headline, with a standfirst, a verified seal and a running ticker.

Picked for most stories.

Breaking

A red alert band with the time in IST, for urgent and developing news that is still being verified.

Picked when the story is urgent, or your brief says breaking.

Numbers Desk

One key figure fills the screen, with what it measures, its context and its source.

Picked when a rate, price or result is the story.

Key Points

An explainer page: the headline, then two to four numbered points that light up one at a time while the voice covers each.

Picked when a scheme, rule or budget has several separate changes.

Quote

The exact words a person said, in large italic type under a gold quotation mark, with their name beneath and the presenter in a circle.

Picked when the story turns on what someone said.

Photo Story

One strong photo fills the frame with a slow push-in, the headline low on the image and the presenter as an inset.

Choose it at script review when the story has a good photo.

Shorts & Reels

A vertical 9:16 video for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. The headline and ticker sit at the top, clear of the app's own buttons, with the presenter below.

Choose it when you file the story.

Design a new look

Start from a template and describe the change. The new look keeps to Dashavani colours and always shows the AI presenter label. It arrives as a draft with a preview; approve it to use it at script review.

    Process

    From raw text to broadcast.

    Nothing is voiced or filmed until a person approves it.

    03

    Hear the voice and watch a free draft. Edit it in Studio if needed.
