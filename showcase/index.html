How it works

  1. Pick a verified story. From the Press Information Bureau wire, or paste your own source.
  2. Review the script. The AI writes it and picks one of six templates. Every number must appear in the source, or it is flagged.
  3. Hear the voice. Recorded once per text, with word-timed subtitles in any of the ten languages.
  4. Approve the draft. Adjust the on-screen text and photo timing, or ask for a change in plain words.
  5. Get the final. With an AI presenter or voice only, in landscape and Shorts & Reels, ready to download or upload to YouTube.

Inside the studio

Built with

Node 22, Vite, React and Tailwind; HyperFrames and ffmpeg for video; Gemini, ElevenLabs and HeyGen. Accounts with owner approval, per-account data separation, rate limits and a test suite that checks every route as every kind of user.
